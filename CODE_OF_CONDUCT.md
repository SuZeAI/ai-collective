# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want AI – Collective to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Welcome newcomers and help them get started.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and when an individual represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **suzeai545@gmail.com** or
[@SuZeAI](https://github.com/SuZeAI). Reports will be reviewed and handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behavior they deem
inappropriate.
